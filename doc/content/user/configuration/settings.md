## Items per page

You can change the number of articles displayed on each page. The default is 12 articles per page.

## Opening articles

Enable **Open articles on the original website** to open article titles and
thumbnails directly on the source website. This preference is saved for your
account and is disabled by default.

The reader icon beside each article's domain still opens the saved article in
wallabag. Opening either the original website or the reader does not mark the
article as read; use the existing read/unread action when you finish reading.

This setting applies to the web application. Mobile clients choose their own
opening behavior.

## Reading speed

wallabag calculates a reading time for each article. You can define
here, through this list, if you are a fast or a slow reader. wallabag
will recalculate the reading time for each article.

## Redirection

Where do you want to be redirected to after marking an article as read?

Each time you'll do some actions (_after marking an article as
read/favorite, after deleting an article, after removing a tag from an
entry_), you will be redirected whether:

-   To the homepage
-   To the current page

## Language

You can change the language of wallabag interface.
